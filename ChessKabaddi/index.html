Tactical Squad Battles

Engage in competitive tactical clashes from 2v2 up to epic 10v10 group warfare!

  • Free 2v2 battles ready out of the box.

Zero-Lag Local Wi-Fi (LAN)

Connect instantly with friends and family over the same Wi-Fi network!

  • Zero internet connection required.

  • Built-in team chat to coordinate battlefield tactics seamlessly!

Captain Leadership & Voting

Squad members propose and vote on moves in real time before the timer runs out!

  • Teammates analyze the board and vote on candidate moves.
  • Captains evaluate live suggestions via team dashboards.
  • Captains execute the final raid move before the buzzer sounds!

Raid Hits, Eliminations & Revivals

  • Capture major opponent pieces to trigger knockout raids against their squad!
  • Keep your major pieces protected to stay in the game.
  • Earn vital Revives with strategic captures to bring fallen teammates back to the battlefield!

Classic Pass-and-Play & Engine

  • Enjoy local 2-Player Pass-and-Play mode on a single shared screen!

TV Control, Themes & Audio

  • 1
    Host or Join One player hosts the squad lobby over local Wi-Fi while teammates discover and join automatically.
  • 2
    Strategize & Propose During your turn, squad members propose their best tactical moves on their screens.
  • 3
    Execute & Raid The Captain picks the winning move to capture high-value targets, knock out rival teammates, and revive fallen allies!
  • 4
    Conquer Corner the opponent king and deliver checkmate for the ultimate squad victory!
