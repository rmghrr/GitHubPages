A cleaner, faster way to organize your television channels.

RmgTv is a Live TV and Channel Management application built specifically for the TV and set-top box ecosystem. If you've ever wanted a more organized way to browse your television channels, this is it.

Available on Amazon Appstore
Important: This app is helpful for those who own TV with tuners such as DVB-S2 and DVB-T2 and have a Live channel list in their TV scanned through native TV app. It also supports the list provided by other apps which register their channels into TvInput service such as Haystak News app.

This app do not pull any list from internet or contain any predefined list of channel URL to display. It operates on existing TV channel list which you already got and also it do not provide any channel scan facility. It acts as a top layer interface only to improve user viewing experience.

Disclaimer & Important Notes

  • Zero Content Provided: This application does not host, provide, or download any IPTV streams, media, or playlists from the internet.
  • Local Management Only: This app functions strictly as a local interface. It securely scans your device to organise and display channels that have already been populated on your hardware via the system's native TvInputService.
  • Promotional Imagery: The splash screen and promotional artwork used are for illustrative purposes only. Please review the actual app screenshots and read this description carefully before purchasing.
  • EPG Availability: Electronic Program Guide (EPG) visibility relies entirely on your TV manufacturer (OEM) granting third-party apps read access to the system's internal Programs database.
  • Channel Logos: Automatic logo matching is currently optimised for select Free-to-Air (FTA) channels in India. Support for additional channels and global regions will be added in future updates.
